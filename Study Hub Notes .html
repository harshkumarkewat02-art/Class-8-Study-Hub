📐 Mathematics


Rational Numbers ＋
  • Learn the meaning of rational numbers.
  • Understand numerator and denominator.
  • Practice addition, subtraction, multiplication and division.
  • Revise important properties.
Linear Equations ＋
  • Understand variables and constants.
  • Learn how to solve simple linear equations.
  • Practice transposition and inverse operations.
Quadrilaterals ＋
  • A quadrilateral has four sides.
  • Sum of interior angles is 360°.
  • Learn properties of different quadrilaterals.

🔬 Science


Crop Production ＋
  • Learn agricultural practices.
  • Study sowing, irrigation and harvesting.
  • Understand Kharif and Rabi crops.
Microorganisms ＋
  • Microorganisms are very small living organisms.
  • Important groups include bacteria, fungi and protozoa.
  • Some microorganisms are useful.
  • Some microorganisms cause diseases.
Coal and Petroleum ＋
  • Coal and petroleum are fossil fuels.
  • They are exhaustible natural resources.
  • Petroleum gives products such as petrol and diesel.

🌍 Social Science


🏛️ History ＋
  • Study important historical events.
  • Make timelines of important dates.
  • Learn important people, places and terms.
🗺️ Geography ＋
  • Study natural and human resources.
  • Learn important geographical terms.
  • Practice important map locations.
⚖️ Civics ＋
  • Understand democracy and government.
  • Learn important constitutional concepts.
  • Study the role of government institutions.
